The unusual part. Microsoft charges $15 a person and includes every agent — its revenue stays flat as agents multiply. We also charge per agent, so we grow as they do. Agents with no human owner fit no seat; no one publicly prices blocking them yet.

Per‑check pricing is a race to the bottom — AWS charges $0.025 per 1,000 — and gives buyers bills they can’t forecast. We include 100,000 checks per person or agent a month. The buyer gets a flat bill; we are paid for safety, not compute.

$25k for 60 days, 100% credited to year one — the buyer risks little. The yearly order form is signed before the pilot starts, so a good pilot becomes a contract without a second sale.

A 1,000‑staff company: 300 people × $25 × 12 = $90k, plus 200 agents × $12 × 12 = $28.8k. $119k a year at list, about $95–100k after the usual discount.

$15 a person is what Microsoft charges for Agent 365. Per‑person prices cluster at $8–15 a month (Obsidian, Harmonic, WitnessAI). Enterprise at $25 has to earn its premium by blocking, not only watching. AWS Marketplace ↗

Specialist AI‑security platforms list at $100–250k a year (Straiker, Pillar, Island). Our $50k minimum sits below what the median company pays CrowdStrike or Zscaler — easy to approve.
